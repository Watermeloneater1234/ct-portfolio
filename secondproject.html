My Second Project

Data Analysis

About This Project

My second term of Computer Technology was a data analysis project in which we were tasked with sorting information in to a spreadsheet and querying it.

My Reflection

Unfortunately, I missed the main week of school, this project was to be completed when i was my duke of ed camp, so my reflection will be what I learned over the term. This term I learned about spreadsheets and ways to analysis data. I learnt to turn raw data into a clean ,organised, structured spreadsheet. This made me realise how big of a difference between the usefulness of raw, unorganized data and the data in spreadsheets. Furthermore, if I made even the slightest mistake it would alter the results. After sorting the data, I had to learn how to utilize the query keys in google sheets. I also had to learn how to use other functions like sum, average, and count to get the data I needed. Throughout the project I mainly used google sheets, along with some research on some functions(some stated above) to sort my data and analyze it. 0verall, even though i missed the main task of the term, I came away with a good understanding of how google sheets and studying data works and I am always willing to learn.
